# Plateau And Liquid Reset

## Plateau Definition

Treat a plateau as:

- No downward change in the 7-day average weight for 2-4 weeks, despite consistent tracking.
- Waist or body-fat measurements also flat.
- Not a normal single-day fluctuation.

Do not call it a plateau after only a few days.

## Before Any Reset

Check and fix these first:

1. Is intake tracking accurate for at least 7 days?
2. Is sleep consistently at least 7 hours?
3. Is water intake near the daily `kg * 30-50 ml` target?
4. Is protein adequate?
5. Has daily movement or training dropped?
6. Is stress, menstrual cycle, salt, or recent travel affecting weight?

Only move to stronger methods when these basics are stable.

## Progressive Plateau Strategies

Use this order:

1. Increase daily steps or low-impact movement.
2. Improve sleep and water consistency.
3. Add one higher-carb refeed meal, not an entire cheat day.
4. Cycle calories: two normal days followed by one slightly lower day.
5. Change training stimulus: add a new exercise, increase load, or change rep range.
6. Use a short structured liquid reset only after the above fail.

## One-Day Liquid Reset

Default to one day only. It is a short reset, not a multi-day fast.

Allowed liquids:

- Water.
- Unsweetened tea or black coffee.
- Clear broth or light vegetable soup.
- Optional: one or two protein shakes or unsweetened dairy drinks to preserve protein.

Example:

```text
Total fluids: weight_kg * 30-50 ml
Optional protein: 60-100 g
Solid food: none
Duration: 1 day
```

Do not make it a zero-protein, zero-electrolyte water-only day for most people.

## Liquid Reset Safety Gates

Do not recommend a liquid reset for:

- Diabetes or blood-sugar instability.
- Kidney, liver, or heart disease.
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- History of eating disorders.
- Low blood pressure, dizziness, or fainting.
- High physical labor or intense training on the same day.
- Users under 18.

For otherwise healthy adults, limit to one day by default. Do not recommend multi-day liquid fasting unless a qualified professional is directly supervising.

## After The Reset

- Break the reset with a small, easy meal: protein plus vegetables.
- Return to the normal calorie plan the next day.
- Do not immediately cut calories further.
- Wait 7-14 days and evaluate the 7-day average before declaring success.

## Stop Immediately

Stop and recommend professional care for:

- Dizziness, fainting, heart palpitations, confusion, or severe weakness.
- Vomiting, persistent diarrhea, or signs of dehydration.
- Severe hunger that triggers binge behavior.
