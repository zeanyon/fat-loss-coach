# Training And Movement

## Aerobic Route Selection

Use joint symptoms as the first decision. Then use BMI.

| Condition | Recommended Start |
|---|---|
| BMI >= 30 or knee/back/ankle pain | Brisk walking, incline walking, stair climbing, elliptical, swimming |
| BMI < 30 and no joint pain | Running, jump rope, swimming |

## Aerobic Parameters

### Running

- Warm up dynamically for 5-10 minutes.
- For a healthy trained adult: target heart rate roughly 140-160 bpm.
- If heart rate is below 140, keep running.
- If heart rate is above 155, switch to walk intervals until it returns.
- Total session: about 60 minutes.
- Finish with static stretching.

Adjust the range when age, fitness, or medical risk makes 140-160 inappropriate. A more conservative range for beginners or large-body users is 120-140 bpm.

### Incline Walking

- Treadmill incline: 12-15.
- Speed: 4-4.5 km/h.
- Start at 20-30 minutes and progress gradually.

### Stair Climbing

- Climb 20-30 floors.
- Take the elevator down to protect joints.
- Complete 5-7 rounds as capacity allows.

For very large body weight, increase aerobic volume gradually, but keep impact low and avoid stacking long sessions on sore joints.

## Anaerobic: Three-Day Split

Use `push / pull / legs` as the default split. Emphasize shoulder and back work more than chest-only work.

For specific exercise choices, images, and Chinese instructions, read `references/exercise-library.md`.

### Home Version

- Push: push-up progression, pike push-up, chair dips.
- Pull: doorway row with towel or band, superman, reverse snow angel.
- Legs: bodyweight squat, split squat, glute bridge, calf raise.

### Gym Version

- Push: dumbbell or barbell press, shoulder press, triceps extension.
- Pull: row, lat pulldown or assisted pull-up, face pull, biceps curl.
- Legs: squat or leg press, Romanian deadlift, lunge, calf raise.

### Progression

- Start with 2-3 sets per movement.
- Use 8-15 repetitions.
- Increase load or reps only when form remains stable.
- Leave 1-2 repetitions in reserve for most sets.

Track effort with either RIR or RPE:

| Measure | Meaning | Fat-loss default |
|---|---|---|
| RIR 2 | Two repetitions left before failure | Good for compound lifts |
| RPE 7 | About 70% of perceived maximum effort | Good for general training |

Use double progression:

1. Work inside a rep range, for example 8-12.
2. When the top of the range is reached with clean form, increase load slightly.
3. Drop back to the low end of the range.
4. If progress stalls for two sessions, reduce volume or load by about 10% for one week.

Do not advance load after missed repetitions or pain.

## Weekly Schedule Default

```text
Day 1: Push + optional low-impact cardio
Day 2: Aerobic session
Day 3: Pull + optional low-impact cardio
Day 4: Rest or light walk
Day 5: Legs
Day 6: Aerobic session
Day 7: Rest, mobility, and posture work
```

## Posture And Face Work

For people losing weight quickly, include:

- Chin tucks, tongue posture awareness, and neck mobility.
- Face and jaw relaxation work, but describe it as muscle tone and posture support, not spot reduction.
- Daily posture resets for rounded shoulders and forward head.

Avoid claiming facial exercises alone prevent all skin sagging. Frame them as supportive habits.
