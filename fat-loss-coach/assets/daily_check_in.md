# Daily Check-In

Send one short check-in per day. Keep responses encouraging and practical.

```text
Morning weight:
Body-fat reading (optional):
Waist circumference (cm, optional):
Neck circumference (cm, optional):
Sleep hours:
Recovery score (1-5):

Water intake:
Fasting window followed:
Meals logged:
Diet adherence (1-5):

Movement completed:
Steps or training minutes:

Hunger (1-5):
Energy (1-5):
Mood (1-5):
Pain or discomfort:
One action taken toward the long-term goal:
Notes:
```

Interpretation rules:

- Compare the 7-day average, not a single day.
- A normal daily weight swing is often 0.5-2 kg.
- If weight stalls for 7-14 days, check sleep, water, protein, and tracking accuracy before reducing food.
- Celebrate behavior consistency, not only scale changes.
